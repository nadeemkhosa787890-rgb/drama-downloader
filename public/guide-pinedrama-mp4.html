How to Download PineDrama Episodes as MP4

PineDrama series are made of short episodes, often 60–90 of them. The app is built for streaming, so if you want an episode as a plain MP4 file on your device — for a flight, a weak-signal area, or just to keep your place in a series — here is the simple way.

Step 1 — Copy the share link

Open the PineDrama app, open the drama (or the exact episode) you want, tap the Share button, and choose "Copy link". A series link works best: one link can resolve the whole collection at once.

Step 2 — Paste it into the resolver

Open the Drama Downloader resolver, paste the link, and tap "Resolve Episodes". You will see the drama's title, its poster, and the full episode list before anything downloads, so you can check you have the right series.

Step 3 — Download one episode, selected episodes, or the full series

Each episode card has its own Download MP4 button with a live progress bar. Tick the episodes you want and use "Download selected", or use "Download Full Series" for everything. Files save with clean names — the drama's full title plus the episode number, like "My Bodyguard is the Supreme Warrior Ep 01.mp4" — so your folder stays in order.

About quality

The downloader always picks the largest MP4 the source serves for each episode and shows its real resolution on the card (for example 1080×1920 or 720×1280). Quality is decided by the source, not by the tool: if a platform only published an episode at 720, no downloader can turn it into a true 1080 — it would only fake the size. What you see on the card is the honest number.

Saving the poster too

The button under each episode's download button saves that episode's thumbnail, cropped to the tall 9:16 ratio at the highest resolution the source provides — handy for covers and collections.

A fair-use note

Download only for your own offline viewing, and only episodes you are allowed to keep. Do not re-upload or redistribute other people's dramas. If an episode refuses to resolve, it is usually paid, private, or region-locked — see our guide on locked and missing episodes.
