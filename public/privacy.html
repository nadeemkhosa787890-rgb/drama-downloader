Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This is a plain-language summary of how this tool handles information; the site owner should review it with their own details before relying on it legally.

What the tool does

When you paste a drama link, our server passes that link to public resolver services to fetch the drama's title, poster, episode list, and available video file addresses, and passes the file to your browser when you choose to download. That is the whole service.

What we store

  • No accounts, no uploads: there is nothing to sign up for and nothing of yours is uploaded or kept.
  • Theme choice: your Bright/Dark preference is stored only in your own browser (local storage) and never sent to us.
  • Server logs: like almost every web server, ours keeps short-lived technical logs (time, page requested, approximate region from IP) for security and abuse prevention. Logs are not used to build profiles and are rotated.
  • No video storage: video files are never stored on this server; they pass through during your download only.

Third parties

Resolving a link requires contacting the source platforms and public resolver services, which receive the link you pasted. If advertising or analytics are added to this site in future, this page will be updated to name them, and the relevant providers' policies will also apply.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's local storage at any time to erase your theme preference. For any privacy question or request, contact us via the Contact page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a fresh date.
