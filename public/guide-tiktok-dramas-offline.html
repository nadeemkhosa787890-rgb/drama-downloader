How to Save TikTok Short Dramas Offline

TikTok's mini-dramas are addictive — and easy to lose. The feed moves on, a series gets split across dozens of clips, and finding episode 34 again can take longer than watching it. Saving the episodes you are allowed to keep, in order, solves that.

Copy the right link

In TikTok, open the drama episode and tap Share → Copy link. If the video belongs to a series or collection, use the series link when one is offered — the resolver can then list the whole set of episodes at once instead of one clip at a time. Short links (vt.tiktok.com) work too; if one ever fails, open it in your browser first and paste the full address it lands on.

Resolve, then choose your episodes

Paste the link into the resolver and tap "Resolve Episodes". You will get the drama's title and poster with every available episode as its own card. From there you can download a single episode, tick several and download them together, or take the full series. Each card shows a live progress percentage while its file downloads.

Names that stay in order

Files are saved as the drama's full title plus the episode number — "Title Ep 01.mp4", "Title Ep 02.mp4" — so a folder of 80 episodes sorts perfectly on any phone or computer, with no renaming by hand.

Quality, honestly

The tool measures the real size of every file the source offers and takes the largest — up to true 1080-class files where the uploader published them. The resolution printed on each episode card is measured from the actual MP4, not guessed from a label.

Keep it personal

Offline copies are for your own viewing. Don't repost full episodes or series to other platforms — that is the creator's work and the creator's right. For the episodes that won't resolve at all, see why some episodes are locked or missing.
