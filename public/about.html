About Drama Downloader

Drama Downloader is a small, free web tool for people who love short dramas. Paste a PineDrama or TikTok drama link, and the tool reads the series' public information — title, poster, episode list — and gives you clean MP4 downloads for the episodes you are allowed to keep, saved in the highest quality the source serves and named in perfect episode order.

What it is

  • A link resolver: it works only with links you paste, one request at a time.
  • Browser-based: nothing to install, works on phone and computer.
  • Private by design: no accounts, and no catalog of dramas is stored or published here.

What it is not

  • Not a streaming site: no videos are hosted, embedded, or stored on this server. Files pass through only at the moment you download them.
  • Not a paywall bypass: paid, private, or region-locked episodes stay locked.
  • Not connected to PineDrama or TikTok: this is an independent tool, and those names belong to their owners.

Our one rule

Downloads are for personal offline viewing. The dramas belong to their creators — enjoy them, keep them for yourself, and don't redistribute them.
